About Target1st

Online CBT mock test platform for Haryana government exams

Target1st is an independent exam-prep platform built for Indian competitive exam aspirants, currently focused on Haryana government exams — HARTRON, HSSC Group D, and similar state-level recruitment tests. We build affordable, exam-aligned online mock tests and study tools so students can practice in a real computer-based-test (CBT) format before their actual exam.

Our Mission

To make quality exam preparation accessible to every student in Haryana and beyond — regardless of city, background, or budget. A ₹99 mock-test bundle made with care beats a ₹2,000 coaching pack made for profit.

What We Offer

  • Full-length CBT mock tests with sectional timers, negative marking, and a real exam-style interface
  • Free demo tests you can try before paying anything
  • Exam-specific bundles: 10 mock tests per exam for a flat price
  • A performance dashboard and public leaderboard to track and compare your progress

Where We're Headed

Target1st started as a mock-test tool, but "prep" is the point — over time we plan to add more study formats beyond just mocks (notes, practice sets, and more) under the same platform, without changing what you've already paid for.
