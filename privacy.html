Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data Target1st collects and how it is used.

Data We Collect

  • At sign-up: Name, email address, and phone number — used to create your account and identify your bundle access.
  • Test activity: Your test attempts, scores, section-wise accuracy, and time taken — stored to power your personal performance dashboard and the leaderboard.
  • Payment data: Processed entirely by Razorpay. We never see or store your card, UPI, or bank account details — only that a payment succeeded, and its payment ID.

How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account, and unlock the exam bundles you purchase.
  • To show you your own performance dashboard (scores, accuracy trend, weak sections).
  • To send occasional updates about new tests or exams (you may opt out anytime by contacting us).
  • We never sell your data to third parties.

Public Leaderboard

When you complete a test, your name and score may appear on that test's leaderboard, visible to other students who take the same test. If you'd prefer not to appear publicly, contact us and we'll look into an option for you.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal browser local storage to save your in-progress test answers and timer state (so a refresh doesn't lose your attempt), and basic filter preferences. We do not use tracking cookies or ad networks.

Data Storage

Your data is stored on Google Firebase infrastructure with access restricted to Target1st's admin account.

Contact

Questions about your data? Reach us via the Contact page.
