What is the SSC CHSL exam pattern?

Tier 1: 100 questions, 200 marks across English, Reasoning, Quant and GA, each with its own 15-minute timer. Tier 2: Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning, English, GA, a qualifying Computer Knowledge module, and a qualifying Typing/Skill Test.

Is there negative marking in SSC CHSL?

Yes — 0.5 marks per wrong answer in Tier 1, and 1 mark per wrong answer in Tier 2 objective sections.

Does Tier 1 count toward the final merit list?

No. Tier 1 is qualifying only — final merit is prepared from Tier 2 Section 1 and Section 2 marks.
